The affordable and sustainable way to furnish your HEC room

Meublation is a student-run platform that enables HEC Paris students to buy, sell, store and donate furniture. Our goal is to reduce furniture waste on campus by ensuring that every piece of furniture is reused.

Meublation was founded in 2019 and is now part of Espr, the ecological association of the campus.

Services

Discover all services of Meublation

01

Buy

Meublation has 2 containers full of items used by former HEC students! You can find everything from kettles to microwaves, lamps, drying racks or carpets. This year, skip Ikea and the trouble of moving big stuff, and come see for yourself the hundreds of furniture pieces waiting for you.

Everything is second hand, so be ready to make good deals.

Come with a Revolut account, as you will need it to pay the seller of your dream furniture. We are only a marketplace (see how "Sell" works to understand).

Our store opens during "Open Days", usually in August, December and May.

02

Sell

Meublation is counting on you to please the next generation of HEC students with your used furniture. Not items that no longer work, of course, but those you cannot take back home.

Come, let us manage your furniture, and you will receive your money when the item is bought!

Please complete the form for each item you sell.

Sell here
03

Store

The "Store" part of Meublation is a bit different from Buy and Sell. We have a room where we store items of current HEC students who are not on campus.

If you are leaving your room for some months but plan to come back to Paris, let us keep your stuff. You can store cases, boxes or furniture for €2 to €4 per month.

Please fill in the form if you want to store your things.
