Read everything you need to know before storing
Storage

We keep your things while you are away

Leaving your room for a few months but coming back to Paris? Meublation stores almost anything for you, as long as it is well packed.

You can store cases, boxes or furniture. Group small items in a box or a bag.

Accepted

Almost all items, as long as they are well packed.

Not accepted

Very large items, such as sofas.

Prices

Simple monthly prices

Small item

€2 per month

For items smaller than 60 × 40 × 40 cm.

Large item

€4 per month

For anything bigger than that, such as a mattress or a table.

Insurance

For now, only furniture is covered. For clothes and other personal belongings, you need to sign a liability waiver.
