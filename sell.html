How it works

Selling in six steps

01

Install Revolut

You will be paid on Revolut, so before selling, install Revolut and get your account verified.

02

Register each item

Fill in the online form. You need one form per item.

03

Pack carefully

Pack your items well, especially the small ones.

04

Drop them off

Leave them in the containers behind building B1, during opening hours.

05

We sell them

Meublation does its best to sell your items to the arriving students.

06

Get paid

After the sale, you are paid directly on your Revolut account.

Go to step 2
Payment

How you get paid

A verified Revolut account is required

Without one, we cannot accept your item.

How does the payment work?

The buyer pays you directly through Revolut. Meublation keeps a 10% commission.

How long can my item stay for sale?

There is no deadline. After one year, Meublation may lower the price of an unsold item. After two years, it can be given to a partner NGO or sold off at a very low price.
